## 📊 Statistics Roadmap & Hands-On Topics

### Module 1: Descriptive Statistics & Probability Basics
* **Core Concepts**: Population vs. Sample, Central Tendencies (Mean, Median, Mode), Percentiles & Quartiles.
* **Outliers & Correlation**: Outlier detection, correlation analysis, and why the mean is sensitive to outliers.
* **Probability & Distributions**:
  * Fundamentals of Probability & Probability Density Functions (PDF)
  * Normal Distribution & Standard Normal Distribution ($Z$-score)
  * Central Limit Theorem (CLT)
* **🧪 Hands-On Lab 1**: EDA, outlier removal, distribution plots, and applying CLT using `numpy`, `pandas`, and `seaborn`.

### Module 2: Inferential Statistics & Hypothesis Testing
* **Foundations**: Descriptive vs. Inferential Statistics, Confidence Intervals, and $p$-value interpretation.
* **Hypothesis Testing Essentials**:
  * Steps in Hypothesis Testing & Decision Criteria
  * Type I ($\alpha$) and Type II ($\beta$) Errors
  * One-tailed vs. Two-tailed Tests
* **Parametric & Non-Parametric Tests**:
  * $Z$-test vs. $T$-test (One-sample, Two-sample, Paired $T$-tests)
  * $F$-test and ANOVA (Analysis of Variance)
  * Chi-Square Test for Independence
* **🧪 Hands-On Lab 2**: Executing ANOVA and Chi-Square tests using `scipy.stats` on real-world datasets.
